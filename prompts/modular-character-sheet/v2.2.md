# SECTION A — FIXED: C0.5 Modular Sheet Technical Contract

## A1. Objective

Create a professional modular 2D anime character asset sheet for deterministic extraction into independent transparent PNG body parts and skeletal animation in Spine 2D.

The sheet contains **ONE original character disassembled into exactly 18 separate body-part assets**. The parts represent one coherent character in one neutral rest pose, not alternative character designs, a turnaround, or a costume catalog.

Prioritize anatomical segmentation, extractability, consistent scale, complete hidden geometry, and skeleton compatibility over decoration.

Do not create a chibi, super-deformed, doll-like, or childlike-proportioned character. The assembled character should be approximately **seven heads tall**, using the proportions of the AIGG C0.5 candidate rig.

## A2. View, anatomical sides, and rest pose

Use exactly one view: **`three_quarter_right`**, facing screen right, with consistent perspective for all parts.

**Near/far convention:** The character's anatomical **right** is the **near** side, closer to the viewer and on the image-left side of the character's body in the C0.5 reference pose. The character's anatomical **left** is the **far** side, farther from the viewer and on the image-right side. `*_near` attachments must be the anatomical-right parts, and `*_far` attachments the anatomical-left parts. Preserve the correct overlap and depth order in the assembled pose. Do not mirror or swap sides.

Use a neutral upright standing pose with arms relaxed and slightly separated from the torso and legs naturally aligned beneath the pelvis. **Draw each detached body part at the orientation it occupies in the assembled neutral pose. Do not rotate pieces to vertical or horizontal merely for arranging the sheet.** No dramatic foreshortening, action poses, independent perspective changes, or arbitrary rotation.

## A3. Mandatory parts — exactly 18

Produce **exactly one separate, complete visual asset** for each named C0.5 required slot:

**Head and body (6):**

1. `hair_back` — the complete rear hairstyle, including normally hidden attachment areas.
2. `head` — a complete head and face, with eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, nose, mouth and ears **painted directly on the head**.
3. `hair_front` — front bangs and frontal hairstyle separated from the head.
4. `neck` — complete neck, including its hidden attachment areas.
5. `torso` — upper body, with upper-body clothing integrated into this part.
6. `hips` — pelvis and waist, with clothing integrated and independent thigh attachment areas.

**Arms (6):**

7. `upper_arm_near`
8. `upper_arm_far`
9. `forearm_near`
10. `forearm_far`
11. `hand_near`
12. `hand_far`

Each arm is independently split into upper arm, forearm and hand. No shared or merged segments. Both hands have **relaxed, slightly open neutral gestures**, consistent with each other and the view. Include sleeves, bracers and gloves within the correct anatomical segments. Do not put an object into the hands.

**Legs (6):**

13. `thigh_near`
14. `thigh_far`
15. `shin_near`
16. `shin_far`
17. `foot_near`
18. `foot_far`

Each leg is independently split into thigh, shin and foot. **Feet and boots are entirely separate from the shins, with the separation at the ankle.** Do not merge the thighs with the hips or with each other.

For this MVP sheet, include **no extra asset regions**. The nine optional C0.5 slots (`tail`, `ear_far`, `ear_near`, `skirt_back`, `skirt_front`, `hair_tail`, `acc_head`, `weapon`, `hand_near_front`) are all intentionally absent. Do not generate content for them.

Do not produce separate eye, iris, eyebrow, eyelash or mouth sprites; additional head views; alternate hands; alternate costumes; duplicated limbs; extra hair pieces; or assembled reference figures.

## A4. Coherent anatomical scale

All 18 components must be part of a single anatomically consistent character at **one shared scale**. Approximate visual guidelines in units of head height:

- Torso about 1.3.
- Hips about 1.1.
- Upper arm about 1.1.
- Forearm about 0.95.
- Hand about 0.65.
- Thigh about 1.55.
- Shin about 1.45.
- Neck artwork about 0.4, including hidden attachment overlap.

These are visual guidelines rather than exact attachment rectangle dimensions or bone lengths. Preserve a coherent seven-head-high assembled figure; never enlarge or shrink one part merely to fill a sheet cell.

`hair_front` and `hair_back` must fit the same head, with the same perspective, color, volume and hairline. Long or voluminous hairstyles may extend well beyond the head; do not enforce an arbitrary hair-width cap. **Any selected hairstyle must be representable using only `hair_front` and `hair_back` for this first MVP.**

## A5. Joint overlap and hidden geometry

Every part must be fully painted even in areas normally concealed by adjacent parts. Provide sufficient continuous art around every pivot for rigid cutout animation.

Target major-joint overlap of roughly **one tenth of the head height**, adjusted to the anatomy and costume; this is a visual approximation of the C0.5 overlap, not an exact pixel measurement.

- Shoulder: upper-arm art extends beneath the torso.
- Elbow: upper arms and forearms have complete, rounded, overlapping painted ends.
- Wrist: forearms and hands overlap cleanly.
- Hip: independent thigh tops extend beneath the pelvis.
- Knee: thighs and shins have completed, rounded overlapping ends.
- Ankle: shins and independent feet/boots overlap cleanly.
- Head/neck: neck extends beneath both head and torso.

Do not cut a segment at its visible joint line; do not leave hollow rings, unpainted tubes, transparent holes, or unfinished attachment ends. Avoid baked shadows from neighboring pieces on surfaces that will rotate independently. Moderate joint rotations should not instantly reveal missing pixels.

## A6. Sheet layout and minimum resolution

Produce one high-resolution sheet, preferably approximately **3072 × 3072 pixels** when supported. Use a predictable layout, without any text or box borders:

- Row 1: `hair_back`, `head`, `hair_front`, `neck`.
- Row 2: `torso`, `hips`, `upper_arm_near`, `upper_arm_far`.
- Row 3: `forearm_near`, `forearm_far`, `hand_near`, `hand_far`.
- Row 4: `thigh_near`, `thigh_far`.
- Row 5: `shin_near`, `shin_far`.
- Row 6: `foot_near`, `foot_far`.

Adjust row heights and cell widths to natural part sizes. Preserve each part's original scale and neutral-pose orientation rather than stretching, shrinking, or rotating it to fit a cell. No assets may touch, overlap, be cropped, or have intersecting **rectangular extraction bounds**. Each part should fit in its own rectangle with fully transparent margins. Target approximately **1/30 of the sheet width** as separation between unrelated parts when practical.

When output resolution is less than 2048 pixels per side, aim for a head height of **at least one eighth of the sheet height**, if all required parts can still fit. Keep hands, feet and face details readable.

If space is constrained, priorities are: (1) all 18 parts exist, (2) coherent anatomy and scale, (3) independent complete joint geometry, (4) adequate pixel detail, (5) non-overlapping rectangular extraction bounds, (6) preferred generous spacing and row order. **Note: violating requirement (5) prevents direct use by the current box-only importer; a different layout or preprocessing would then be needed.**

Do not print labels, numbers, row guides, bounding rectangles, grid lines, watermarks or signatures. Do not add a preview of the assembled character.

## A7. Genuine PNG transparency

Output a real **RGBA PNG**. Background pixels outside the assets must have **alpha 0**. Solid interior pixels of the painted parts should have **alpha 255**. Use partial alpha only for legitimate antialiased silhouettes and intentionally transparent internal material details.

No checkerboard painted into the image, colored haze, detached stray pixels, background tint, glows, color splashes, ground shadows, or semi-transparent background textures. Prevent red, cyan, yellow or gray fringes in empty areas. All visible artwork must be confined to the 18 isolated part regions.

Actual PNG alpha will be validated programmatically; following this text alone is not proof of correct transparency.

## A8. Costume division and visual consistency

Treat these parts as pieces of **one finished illustration that has been separated at anatomical joints**. Every part must match the same costume construction, body, perspective, lighting, line weight, palette, materials and shading.

Attach clothing to the anatomical segment that moves with it: torso armor to `torso`; sleeve sections to their corresponding upper arms/forearms; gloves to hands; trouser sections to thighs/shins; boots to feet; waistband and belt detail to `hips`.

For this first MVP, avoid independently swinging capes, skirts, ribbons, equipment, weapons, additional hair strands or floating detachable accessories: they have no allocated part regions here. Fixed decorations may be painted directly on the correct body part without creating an additional asset.

No separate detachable costume layers. No added skeleton slots.

## A9. Safety and presentation

The character must be unmistakably adult. Clothing should be modest and non-sexualized, with no exaggerated emphasis on the chest, hips or buttocks. Preserve a high-quality anime game-art aesthetic with clean line art, coherent cel shading and consistent illumination.

Do not generate chibi proportions, a turnaround, a collage, extra body parts, alternate expressions, extra poses, or a fully assembled character.

## A10. Final visual checklist

Before finalizing, check:

1. Exactly 18 distinct required regions, with no missing or duplicated parts.
2. All six arm and six leg segments are separate; feet are not fused with shins.
3. Complete face painted on `head`; hair separated into front and back.
4. Seven-head-tall adult anatomy and consistent one-scale proportions.
5. Anatomical-right = near = image-left; anatomical-left = far = image-right.
6. Same three-quarter-right view and neutral-pose component orientations.
7. Complete hidden geometry and overlapping painted joint ends.
8. Rectangular extraction areas do not intersect, and no pieces touch.
9. True transparent background; no haze or stray pixels.
10. No extra alternate artwork or reference preview.
11. Costume, palette and light match across all pieces.

Final output: a single, clean, modular sheet of exactly 18 independent 2D anime character parts for deterministic extraction and attachment to the C0.5 Spine skeleton.
